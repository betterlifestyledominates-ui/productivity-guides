A Simple Morning Routine That Takes 15 Minutes

A good morning routine is 3 to 5 small habits you repeat in the same order every day. Most routines fail because they are too big, so start small.

What to include

  • Wake up at the same time to help your body clock settle.
  • Drink a glass of water after hours without fluids.
  • Move for a few minutes by stretching or getting daylight.
  • Pick one top priority for the day.

Make it stick

Attach each habit to something you already do, prepare the night before, and restart the next day if you miss one.
