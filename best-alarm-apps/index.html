Best Alarm Apps: A Simple Guide to Waking Up on Time

If you keep hitting snooze, the problem usually isn't willpower. A basic phone alarm is built to be ignored. The right alarm app makes it harder to fall back asleep and easier to wake up feeling normal.

Best alarm apps, by problem

  • Alarmy: best for heavy sleepers. The alarm won't stop until you complete a task, such as solving a math problem, scanning a barcode, or taking a photo of a spot in your home.
  • Sleep Cycle: best for gentle wake-ups. It wakes you during a lighter sleep phase inside a time window you choose, instead of at one exact minute.
  • Sleep as Android: best for Android users who want control. It offers smart wake-up, many ways to dismiss the alarm, and detailed sleep tracking.
  • Google Clock or Apple Clock: best free option. Reliable, already on your phone, and no setup is needed.

Quick tips for any alarm app

  • Keep your phone across the room so you have to stand up.
  • Set one alarm instead of five.
  • Go to bed at a consistent time, because no app can fix too little sleep.
  • Give any new alarm at least seven days before judging it.
